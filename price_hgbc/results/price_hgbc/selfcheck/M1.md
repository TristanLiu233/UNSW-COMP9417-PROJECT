## 7. Remaining risks

* **Untuned model configuration.** M1 uses sklearn defaults. Any later hyper-parameter search must use 2013 (or earlier)
  validation only, never 2014.
* **τ weakly identified.** The τ optimum on 2013 is flat (±0.03 pp over 0.47–0.51). Do not over-interpret τ = 0.48, and
  expect the best τ to move under label-share drift (class-1 share rose from 43.7 % in 2011 to 48.6 % in 2014).
* **One evaluation year.** 2014 is a single year, and the group already used 2014 for its own ablation and model selection.
  My M1 feature list was fixed in advance, not chosen on 2014, but later comparisons on 2014 should remember that it is not fully "fresh" project-wide.
* **Cross-platform.** Repeatability is confirmed on this macOS-arm64 environment only. Marking runs on Linux. M1 avoids the
  sin/cos columns behind W2, but its Linux identity has not been tested. The marking output only needs to be valid and
  deterministic on Linux, not bit-equal to macOS.
* **Holidays not used.** NSW public holidays are allowed but are not in the specified M1 feature list.
