## 1. Strategies and versions

| Strategy | Definition | Retrains | τ selection |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S0** | M5 frozen model (`m5/stage1_model.joblib`, `bca5291b…`) for every day | 0 | REF 0.45 |
| **S1** | expanding window (2010-01-29 … D); retrain at the end of 2016, 2018, 2020, 2022, 2024; effective 1 Jan of the next year | 5 | nested (below) |
| **S2** | same dates; window = the three calendar years (D − 3 y, D]; earlier days only feed lag features | 5 | nested |
| **S3** | CUSUM-triggered (M4 option A, h = 6, k = 0.5, q99/μ₀/σ₀ from the config); S1's window and τ method; cooldown ≥ 180 days between retrain alarm days; budget 6; S reset on every alarm day and after every retrain; S = 0 on 2015-01-01 | 6 (budget used up) | nested |

**Nested τ selection** (same for S1/S2/S3):
1. fit a tuning model (REF params) on window days ≤ D − 1 year;
2. predict the last 12 months (D − 1 year, D] out of sample;
3. pick τ with P1's protocol (0.40/0.45/0.50/0.55/0.60, max BalAcc, ties → 0.5);
4. refit the final model on the whole window; it is used from D+1.

Reference: μ₀ = 0.4435, σ₀ = 0.1988, q99 = 1.0494; by design 1 % of days exceed q99.

**WARN: budget exhausted early.** All 6 retrains are used by 2019-03-17, and the detector then raises 74 more alarms in
2019–2024 that cannot act. Evidence:
* the mean standardised error stays above 0 in every year (z from +0.03 to +0.98);
* the S3 error level is above μ₀ even right after retrains, so S drifts upward by roughly (z − k) per day whenever z > 0.5;
* in 2022 the q99 cap binds on 9.6 % of days instead of 1 %.

Likely causes:
1. μ₀/σ₀ come from REF's 2014 out-of-sample errors (an older model, 2014 market). The post-2015 market is structurally
   harder (higher price volatility, more negative and spike days, falling class-1 share), so the 2014 "normal" is too low;
2. option A never adapts the reference, so after a retrain the new model is still judged against 2014;
3. h = 6 was calibrated for ARL₀ ≈ 912 days on 2014 behaviour, but later years have higher error and stronger
   autocorrelation, so the real false-alarm rate is much higher.

Does retraining help in the short term (`option_a_retrain_effect.csv`)? Mean log loss over the 60 days after each new
model takes effect, compared with S0 on the same days: −0.008, +0.013, −0.041, −0.117, −0.065, −0.192. After the first
two retrains the gains grow, i.e. the retrains do reduce error against the frozen model.

**Suggested follow-up sensitivity runs** (not done; option A results kept as the official M6 result):
* option B (reference re-estimated after each retrain);
* a larger h or k (e.g. h = 8, k = 0.75), checked against ARL₀ on 2015–2016 only;
* a longer cooldown (e.g. 365 days) to spread the budget;
* a reference taken from the Stage 1 model's own 2015 errors.

Any of these must be pre-registered and chosen without looking at 2025–2026 scores.
